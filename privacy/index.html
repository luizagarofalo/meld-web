Meld — Privacy Policy

What happens between you two stays between you two.

Meld is built so the app itself never has to be trusted with your answers — because it never sees them. This page explains exactly what that means in practice.

Effective September 9, 2026

Everything you and your partner do inside Meld — names, sessions, answers, "Our list" — is saved only on your own device, using Apple's on-device storage. It is never uploaded to us or to any server we run, because we don't run one.

We use one outside service, RevenueCat, and only to process subscription purchases through Apple's App Store. It never sees your names or your answers.

Meld has no ad tracking, no analytics SDK, and nothing that identifies you to a third party. There is no account and no login.

What Meld stores, and where

Meld stores the following, using Apple's SwiftData framework, entirely on your device:

  • The two names you enter, so the app knows whose turn it is
  • Your session history — which decks you've opened and when
  • Your answers and reveals from each session
  • "Our list" — the things that became visible between you as a couple

None of this data is synced to iCloud, sent to a server, or shared with anyone — including us. If you delete Meld, this data is deleted with it. Settings also gives you a direct way to clear "Our list" or delete everything Meld has stored, at any time.

The one thing that does leave your device

To sell subscriptions through Apple's App Store, Meld uses RevenueCat to process purchases and verify your entitlement to premium content. This is the only data that travels off your device, and it's limited to what a purchase requires:

Shared with
RevenueCat, Inc. — subscription infrastructure used by Meldrevenuecat.com/privacy
What's shared
Purchase history and an anonymous device-linked purchaser IDNever your names, answers, sessions, or "Our list"
Used for
Confirming your purchase and unlocking premium content — nothing else
Tracking
None. This data is not linked to your identity or used to track you across apps

What we don't do

  • We don't run analytics, crash reporting, or ad-tracking SDKs of any kind
  • We don't collect your email, phone number, or any account credential — Meld has no account
  • We don't share, sell, or rent any data to advertisers or data brokers
  • We don't have access to your on-device data, even if you asked us to look — it never reaches us

Children's privacy

Meld is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 17.

Changes to this policy

If Meld's data practices change, this page will change with them, and the "Effective" date at the top will update. Practices that would add server-side storage, accounts, or tracking are not currently planned.
