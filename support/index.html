Meld — Support

Here if something isn't working, or you're not sure how something works.

Meld has no account and no cloud, so most issues live on your device — but if anything is confusing, broken, or missing, write to us directly.

Contact

luiza.garofalo@gmail.com

We read every message and reply as soon as we can.

Before you write in

  • Make sure you're on the latest version of Meld from the App Store
  • If a purchase (Meld Pass) didn't unlock correctly, try Settings → Restore purchase first
  • If something looks wrong after an update, try closing and reopening the app

If none of that helps, email us with a short description of what happened — that's the fastest way for us to help.
